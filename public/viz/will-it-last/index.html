The story 1 of 5

Five gigabytes a month

Lina’s phone plan gives her 5 GB of mobile data for every 30 days. On day 1 the counter starts again at zero.

A little every day

Maps on the way to class, music on the tram, a cat video at lunch. Every day adds about the same brick of data, and Lina never checks.

Day 17

On day 17 she finally looks. The phone says 3 GB used, with 13 days to go.

Will it last?

If the 5 GB run out before day 30, her phone crawls like a snail until the month is over. Will they last?
